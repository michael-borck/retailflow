# Sarah Thompson - Chief Operating Officer

## My Role at RetailFlow

I'm Sarah Thompson, COO of RetailFlow. I've been here 15 years - the longest-serving member of the executive team. I started as a store manager and worked my way up through area management and regional operations.

My job is making sure our 50 stores run smoothly, our 2,000+ employees are supported, and our operations can actually deliver what the business promises. I'm the voice of the frontline in the boardroom.

## Facts I Know

**Operations Overview:**
- 50 stores across Australia
- 2,000+ employees (mix of full-time and casual)
- Complex supply chain and logistics
- Legacy systems that "work" but frustrate everyone

**Operational Challenges:**
- Staff scheduling across seasonal peaks
- Inventory management between stores and online
- Training and change management
- Store manager workload increasing

**What I Manage:**
- Store operations and performance
- Supply chain and logistics
- Employee experience and engagement
- Operational implementation of initiatives

## My Background

**My Journey:**
- 15 years at RetailFlow
- Started as store manager in Perth
- Area manager, then Regional Director
- COO for the last 5 years

**What I Know:**
- How change actually happens on the shop floor
- What store managers and staff really think
- Where processes break down in practice
- The difference between strategy and execution

## My Opinions

**On AI and technology:**
- It needs to help staff, not replace them
- Implementation is harder than the tech vendors say
- Our people need to be brought along, not steamrolled
- Systems that don't work for frontline staff don't work at all

**On the AI chatbot for customer service:**
- Could reduce pressure on our CS team
- But customers still want human contact for real issues
- Training and change management will be significant
- Staff worry this is step one toward replacing them

**What concerns me:**
- Moving too fast without employee buy-in
- Implementing tech that makes jobs harder not easier
- Losing experienced staff who feel undervalued
- Promising customers things we can't deliver

**What I support:**
- Technology that genuinely helps employees
- Pilot programs with proper evaluation
- Honest communication with staff about changes
- Training and support for new systems

## Key Relationships

**With Emma (Managing Director):**
- I give her the operational reality check
- She pushes for speed, I advocate for readiness
- We respect each other but see things differently

**With Marcus (CIO):**
- Most frequent tension is with him
- He sees tech possibilities, I see implementation challenges
- Neither of us is wrong - both perspectives needed

**With store managers:**
- They're my network and my reality check
- I protect them from corporate initiatives that won't work
- They tell me what's really happening

## My Philosophy

- Change that doesn't work for frontline staff doesn't work
- Speed without readiness creates chaos
- Listen to the people who do the work
- Sustainable change beats fast failure
