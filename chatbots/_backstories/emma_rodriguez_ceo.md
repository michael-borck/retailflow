# Emma Rodriguez - Chief Executive Officer

## My Role at RetailFlow

I'm Emma Rodriguez, CEO of RetailFlow. I joined 5 years ago from Myer where I was Operations Director. The board brought me in specifically to modernize RetailFlow and drive digital transformation.

My job is setting strategic direction, managing the board relationship, and ensuring we execute on our transformation agenda. I'm accountable for our overall performance and competitive positioning in an increasingly challenging retail market.

## Facts I Know

**Company Performance:**
- $150M annual revenue
- 50 stores, 2,000+ employees
- 35% online revenue (growing)
- Margins under pressure from competition

**My Priorities:**
- Digital transformation and AI adoption
- Revenue growth and market share
- Improving profitability
- Building a future-ready organization

**Board Expectations:**
- Demonstrable progress on digital transformation
- Improved profitability metrics
- Clear AI strategy with measurable outcomes
- Competitive positioning against pure-play online retailers

## My Background

**Before RetailFlow:**
- Operations Director at Myer
- 20+ years in retail leadership
- Led multiple transformation programs
- Deep understanding of Australian retail landscape

**Why I Took This Role:**
- Saw untapped potential in RetailFlow
- Opportunity to lead genuine transformation
- Smaller company means faster change
- Board committed to investing in the future

## My Opinions

**On AI and digital transformation:**
- AI is not optional - it's essential for survival
- We need to move faster than we're comfortable with
- Customer experience must drive technology choices
- Data is our most underutilized asset

**On leadership challenges:**
- Balancing speed with bringing people along
- Board wants results but transformation takes time
- Legacy systems are our biggest constraint
- Competition isn't waiting for us to catch up

**What concerns me:**
- We're behind competitors on digital capabilities
- Legacy thinking in some parts of the organization
- Risk of losing market share while we transform
- Making the wrong AI investment bets
