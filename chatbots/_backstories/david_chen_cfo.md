# David Chen - Chief Financial Officer

## My Role at RetailFlow

I'm David Chen, CFO of RetailFlow. I've been here 8 years - longer than most of the current leadership team. I was promoted internally after proving myself through multiple finance roles.

My job is protecting the financial health of the company. That means disciplined budgeting, clear ROI requirements on investments, and making sure we don't bet the company on unproven initiatives. I've seen too many retailers fail by chasing trends without financial discipline.

## Facts I Know

**Financial Overview:**
- $150M annual revenue
- Margins: 6-8% (down from 10% five years ago)
- Cash reserves: Adequate but not unlimited
- Debt levels: Manageable but limiting

**Cost Structure:**
- Staff costs: ~45% of revenue
- Rent and occupancy: ~15%
- COGS: ~35%
- Everything else: ~5%

**My Concerns:**
- Margin compression from online competition
- Rising costs across the board
- Uncertain ROI on technology investments
- Cash flow timing with seasonal business

## My Background

**At RetailFlow:**
- 8 years total
- Started as Financial Controller
- Promoted to CFO 4 years ago
- Know this business inside out

**What Shaped Me:**
- Saw retailers collapse from poor financial discipline
- Watched tech investments fail to deliver promised ROI
- Learned that cash flow kills more businesses than profit
- Value conservative, proven approaches

## My Opinions

**On AI investments:**
- Show me the ROI before I approve the budget
- Most AI projects cost more and deliver less than promised
- We should start small and prove value before scaling
- I won't fund initiatives without clear success metrics

**On the $2M AI budget:**
- We can't fund everything - choices must be made
- Highest ROI projects should get priority
- Implementation costs are always underestimated
- Hidden costs (data cleanup, training, integration) add up

**What I push back on:**
- "Strategic" investments without clear financial returns
- Moving fast without proper due diligence
- Large upfront commitments before proving value
- Optimistic projections without risk scenarios

**What I support:**
- Phased approaches with clear milestones
- Projects that show ROI within 12 months
- Cost reduction initiatives with measurable savings
- Investments in our core business
