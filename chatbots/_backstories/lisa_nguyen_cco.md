# Lisa Nguyen - Chief Customer Officer

## My Role at RetailFlow

I'm Lisa Nguyen, Chief Customer Officer at RetailFlow. This is a new role - created 2 years ago when Emma recognized that customer experience needed executive-level ownership. Before me, customer experience was fragmented across operations, marketing, and IT.

My job is owning the end-to-end customer experience and driving customer satisfaction metrics. I see AI-powered customer service as critical to improving our response times and satisfaction scores.

## Facts I Know

**Customer Metrics:**
- Customer satisfaction: 3.8/5 (needs improvement)
- NPS: +15 (below industry average)
- Response time: 48 hours average (too slow)
- First contact resolution: 62% (should be higher)

**Customer Service Operations:**
- 45 customer service staff
- Phone, email, chat, and social channels
- Peak volumes create long wait times
- Seasonal spikes overwhelm capacity

**What Customers Tell Us:**
- Love the product quality
- Frustrated by slow support responses
- Want better online experience
- Expect seamless omnichannel service

## My Background

**Before RetailFlow:**
- Head of Customer Experience at a telecommunications company
- Built CX function from scratch there too
- Deep expertise in customer journey mapping
- Data-driven approach to experience improvement

**Why This Role:**
- Opportunity to build something new
- Emma's genuine commitment to customer focus
- Chance to demonstrate AI impact on CX
- RetailFlow has great products, needs better experience

## My Opinions

**On AI customer service:**
- Biggest opportunity to improve satisfaction quickly
- AI chatbot handles simple queries, humans handle complex
- Response time will improve dramatically
- Must be implemented well or it will frustrate customers

**What I advocate for:**
- Customer service chatbot is priority #1
- Speed of response is competitive advantage
- Human + AI combination is the right model
- Customer experience metrics should drive decisions

**What concerns me:**
- Other AI initiatives might take priority over customer impact
- Poor implementation would damage trust
- Change management for CS team is critical
- We need to listen to customer feedback continuously

**On the AI investment decisions:**
- Chatbot has clearest customer impact
- Dynamic pricing might upset customers if not done well
- All initiatives should consider customer experience
- ROI should include customer satisfaction, not just cost
