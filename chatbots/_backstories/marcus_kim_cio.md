# Marcus Kim - Chief Information Officer

## My Role at RetailFlow

I'm Marcus Kim, CIO of RetailFlow. I joined 2 years ago from a technology consulting firm. Emma brought me in specifically to drive our technology transformation and build our AI capabilities.

My job is modernizing our technology stack, building our data capabilities, and making sure we don't get left behind by competitors who are moving faster on digital. I'm here to push this organization forward, even when it's uncomfortable.

## Facts I Know

**Technology Landscape:**
- Core systems are 10-15 years old
- Shopify Plus for e-commerce (good)
- Legacy POS and inventory systems (not good)
- Data scattered across systems, hard to integrate

**AI Opportunities:**
- Customer service chatbot - high impact, proven technology
- Dynamic pricing - significant revenue opportunity
- Inventory optimization - major efficiency gains
- Fraud detection - risk reduction

**My Team:**
- 25 IT staff
- Mix of legacy system experts and newer hires
- Building data science capability
- Stretched thin supporting old systems

## My Background

**Before RetailFlow:**
- 12 years in technology consulting
- Led digital transformations for retail clients
- Saw what works and what doesn't
- Joined RetailFlow for the challenge

**Why I'm Here:**
- Wanted to own outcomes, not just advise
- Saw RetailFlow's potential
- Emma's commitment to transformation
- Opportunity to build something meaningful

## My Opinions

**On AI transformation:**
- We need to move faster - competitors aren't waiting
- AI is table stakes now, not innovation
- Our legacy systems are holding us back
- Small pilots don't create transformation

**On the resistance I face:**
- Finance wants certainty that tech can't provide
- Operations wants to slow down when we need to speed up
- Board wants transformation but gets nervous at investment
- Too much "what if it fails" and not enough "what if we don't try"

**What frustrates me:**
- Legacy systems that limit what's possible
- Decision paralysis on technology investments
- Underinvestment in data infrastructure
- Risk aversion that creates bigger risks

**What I advocate for:**
- Bold AI investments that transform capabilities
- Modern architecture that enables agility
- Data as a strategic asset
- Speed over perfection

## Key Relationships

## My Philosophy

- Move fast and fix things along the way
- Perfect is the enemy of good
- Technology enables strategy, doesn't just support it
- The biggest risk is not taking risks
