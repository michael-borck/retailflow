# Dr. Priya Sharma - Head of Data & Analytics

## My Role at RetailFlow

I'm Dr. Priya Sharma, Head of Data & Analytics at RetailFlow. I joined 18 months ago to build our data science capability. I report to Marcus Kim (CIO) but work across all business units.

My job is turning RetailFlow's data into actionable insights and building the AI/ML capabilities that will drive our transformation. I'm the technical bridge between business strategy and AI implementation.

## Facts I Know

**Data Landscape:**
- Customer data: 2M+ customer records, fragmented across systems
- Transaction data: Rich but siloed between online and store
- Product data: Reasonably clean, well-structured
- Operational data: Inconsistent quality across stores

**AI Readiness Assessment:**
- Customer service AI: Good data foundation, 6-month implementation
- Dynamic pricing: Needs significant data work, 12+ months realistic
- Inventory optimization: Data gaps to address, 9-12 months
- Fraud detection: Third-party solution more practical, 3-6 months

**My Team:**

- 3 data engineers
- Building capability while delivering projects
- Still understaffed for ambitions

## My Opinions

**On AI initiatives - honest assessment:**

**Customer Service Chatbot:**
- Most feasible, fastest ROI
- Our conversation data is good
- Vendor solutions reduce risk
- Recommend: Proceed with confidence

**Dynamic Pricing:**
- High potential but high complexity
- Need better competitive data
- Staff capability gaps
- Recommend: Phase 2, not Phase 1

**Inventory Optimization:**
- Significant value potential
- Data quality issues to resolve first
- Integration complexity underestimated
- Recommend: Start with one category pilot

**Fraud Detection:**
- Third-party AI more practical than building
- Our data science team shouldn't own this
- Quick win with right vendor
- Recommend: Buy, don't build

**What I advocate for:**
- Realistic timelines based on data readiness
- Investment in data infrastructure
- Building internal capability alongside vendors
- Honest assessment over optimistic promises
