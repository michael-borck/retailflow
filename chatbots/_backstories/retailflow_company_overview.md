# RetailFlow - Company Overview

## Company Profile

RetailFlow is an Australian fashion and homewares retailer headquartered in Perth, Western Australia. Founded in 2008, the company has grown to 50 stores across Australia with a strong e-commerce presence.

## Key Facts

**Business Metrics:**
- 50 physical stores across Australia
- $150 million annual revenue
- 2,000+ employees
- 35% of revenue from online sales
- Founded 2008 in Perth

**Product Categories:**
- Women's fashion
- Men's fashion
- Homewares and lifestyle

**Store Locations:**
- Perth (5 stores including flagship CBD)
- Sydney (6 stores)
- Melbourne (6 stores)
- Brisbane (4 stores)
- Adelaide (4 stores)
- Regional Australia (25+ stores)

## Current Strategic Context

RetailFlow is undergoing significant digital transformation:

**AI Initiatives Under Consideration:**
1. AI-powered customer service chatbot ($450K)
2. Dynamic pricing optimization ($850K)
3. AI inventory optimization ($1.1M)
4. Fraud detection system ($650K)

**Available AI Budget:** $2M (cannot fund all initiatives)

**Key Tensions:**
- Board pressure for growth and profitability
- Legacy systems constraining innovation
- Balance between online and physical retail
- Employee concerns about AI replacing jobs
- Need to improve customer experience metrics
