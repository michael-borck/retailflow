Implementation

Our standard deployment is 10–12 weeks. The engine connects to your Shopify Plus catalogue and POS, ingests a competitor price feed, and begins autonomous optimisation. Out of the box the engine runs in fully autonomous mode to maximise speed-to-value.

Technical prerequisites

To reach the modelled performance, the engine requires a clean, structured, SKU-matched competitor price feed refreshed at least daily. PriceLogic does not source this feed; the client provides connectivity to its existing competitive-data service.

Configuration notes

  • Price guardrails (floors, ceilings, max change per cycle) are available but disabled by default to avoid constraining the optimiser. They can be enabled in the admin console.
  • The backtest above was generated against RetailFlow's own historical transaction data; live performance will vary with market conditions.
  • Competitor-feed quality directly determines model accuracy; degraded or missing feeds fall back to inventory-and-demand-only pricing.
