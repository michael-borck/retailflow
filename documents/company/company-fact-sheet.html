Store network

Our 50 stores span every mainland capital plus a strong regional footprint:

  • Perth (WA): 5 stores, including our flagship CBD store
  • Sydney (NSW): 6 stores
  • Melbourne (VIC): 6 stores
  • Brisbane (QLD): 4 stores
  • Adelaide (SA): 4 stores
  • Regional: 25+ stores across regional WA, NSW, VIC, QLD and SA

What we sell

RetailFlow trades across three core categories:

  1. Women's fashion
  2. Men's fashion
  3. Homewares & lifestyle

Our values

Everything we do is anchored in six values: Customer First, Team Spirit, Quality, Innovation, Integrity and Sustainability.

Technology snapshot

Our e-commerce platform runs on Shopify Plus and performs well. In-store point-of-sale and inventory systems are 10–15 years old and are a known constraint. Online and store data currently sit in separate silos. Our IT function comprises roughly 25 staff.
